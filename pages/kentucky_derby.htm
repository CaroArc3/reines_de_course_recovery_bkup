*La Troienne Influence In Derby Winners

---Ron Parker

One of the most overlooked aspects of pedigrees in classic winners is the enduring presence of super mare *La Troienne (*Teddy-Helene de Trois [FR], by Helicon [GB], 1926) whose influence is still strongly felt in the most modern of pedigrees.

The table below shows this influence in the 2004 Kentucky Derby contenders.  The *La Troienne Source column specifies which of her nine fillies and/or two colts each horse carries, while the S or D column reflects whether this influence comes from the Sire or Dam side of the horse's pedigree.

For comparison, the table below that is a study of the past 15 Derby winners, similarly reflecting their *La Troienne influence.

In an era when pedigrees are glossed over by simple statistics such as easily obtainable sire data, how many winners the dam had, what a horse cost, or the limitations of Dosage numbers, it behooves anyone who truly wants to analyze pedigrees to dig deep into the family to understand and appreciate the historical matings that filter down into any given thoroughbred.

The Contenders list will be regularly updated as May approaches, adding and deleting as the situation dictates.  If there's a contender you think should be included, drop us an e-mail (rontb@earthlink.net).  And if, by some chance, a sharp-eyed pedigree student notices an influence we failed to include, let us know.  After all, if you carry a horses' pedigree out to eight generations you're looking at 510 names.  That's an awful lot of eye strain for anyone.
