About This Site

Ellen Parker, who Leon Rasmussen once dubbed as “Round Table’s Boswell”, has been a pedigree analyst and turf writer for most of her adult life.  She created the Reine-de-Course (“Queens of the Turf) to recognize the influence of female families when no such recognition existed at the time.

Ron Parker has been writing about Thoroughbreds–both seriously and humorously– in various publications for half a century.

Both are long-time members of the National Turf Writers and Broadcasters organization.

2020 Kentucky Derby Pedigree Insights

Baby Steps - Ellen Parker

Is the world suffering from a pandemic or is it merely ‘culling’ like a horse owner asking his broodmare band what they have done for him lately? The homepage used that question to frame a very strange classic season and to ask what kind of stallions might emerge from it.

The discussion returned to the site’s core habits of reading pedigrees through families, outcross patterns, and durable female influence: Seattle Slew, Mr. Prospector, Myrtlewood, Round Table, and the deeper Reine lines all remained central to the argument.

The End of Thoroughbred Wagering

Ron Parker

The late homepage also carried Ron Parker’s argument that Thoroughbred racing was being ignored even while bettors were offered every possible novelty market during the pandemic. It sat beside a broader commentary section that defended family-based pedigree thinking against formula breeding and short-term fashion.

Those pieces made the final home page feel current, argumentative, and recognizably local to this archive rather than like another bare index page.
