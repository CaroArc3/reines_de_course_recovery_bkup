Top Contenders For 2005

In Alphabetical Order

AFLEET ALEX (FL)              $ 2,165,800             Best Beyer 112 (Preakness)    

Northern Afleet—Maggy Hawk, by Hawkster

DP 5-0-9-0-0=14     DI 2.11     CD 0.71     Experimental: 124#

Positives: Dam lacks Raise A Native and Northern Dancer.  Ran his usual consistent race in the Derby, ran that much better in the Preakness.

Negatives: Pedigree full of grass influences – Nureyev, Tentam, Silver Hawk, Hawaii – seems better suited to this surface.

*La Troienne influence: Belle of Troy 7S

 

ANDROMEDA’S HERO (KY)        $ 180,270     Best Beyer 94 (Arkansas Derby) 

Fusaichi Pegasas—Marozia, by Storm Bird

DP 12-4-18-0-0=34    DI 2.78    CD 0.82      Experimental: None

Positives:  Tail female to newly-appointed Reine-de-Course Mahubah.  X3 to Almahmoud.

Negatives:  Soundness factor from sire, Storm Bird

*La Troienne influence: None

 

A. P. ARROW (KY)              $ 39,025                     Best Beyer 86 (MSW)         

A. P. Indy—Garimpeiro, by Mr. Prospector

DP 15-6-15-0-0=36              DI 3.80            CD 1.00         Experimental: None

Positives:  From the Reine-de-Course family that got Slew o’ Gold and Aptitude from Seattle Slew line.  Owns Seattle Slew/Mr. Prospector cross that doubles three major families.

Negatives:  Is in serious need of seasoning.

*La Troienne influence:  From sire’s contribution only – Baby League x2/Businesslike.

 

CHEKHOV (KY)          $ 43,966                  Best Beyer 96 (Sham Stakes)

Pulpit—In My Cap, by Vice Regent

DP 8-3-13-0-0=24                DI 2.69            CD 0.79         Experimental: None

Positives:  From tough old Tuscan Red family, lots of *La Troienne and x3 Somethingroyal.  Sire is having a good year.

Negatives:  When asked a serious question in the Peter Pan, he came up empty.

*La Troienne influence:    Baby League x2/Businesslike x2.  The Pulpits are not very sound.

 

GIACOMO (KY)           $ 1,966,316            Best Beyer 100 (Kentucky Derby) 

Holy Bull—Set Them Free, by Stop The Music

DP 5-5-6-0-0=16     DI 4.33     CD 0.94     Experimental: 122#

Positives: Wonderful wake-up call for the breed – he is sans Northern Dancer and Raise A Native.  A real treat for fans of his marvelous sire.

Negatives: Needs to go on proving himself for awhile. Tail-female line quite grassy, might move up on that surface.

*La Troienne influence: Bimelech 6S; Big Event 6S

 

INDY STORM (KY)                $ 40,964        Best Beyer 88 (Allowance)

A. P. Indy—Unbridled Wind, by Unbridled

DP 7-5-12-0-2=26                DI 2.25            CD 0.58         Experimental: None

Positives:  Tail-female to Reine-de-Course Beaming Beauty.  Fifth dam a half sister to Damascus, dam a (minor) stakes winning full sister to ill-fated champion Beaming Beauty.  Linebred to Aloe (Feola/Aroma) and to Marchetta via Turn-to, My Babu, Nato II.

Negatives:  Has not made his presence felt in stakes company to date.

*La Troienne influence:  x6 via Baby League x2/Businesslike x2/Bimelech x2.

 

NOLAN’S CAT (KY)         $ 26,450        Best Beyer 88 (Maiden Special)               

Catienus—Single Goer, by Easy Goer

DP 8-2-10-1-1=22                DI 2.14            CD 0.68         Experimental: None

Positives:  Tail-female to Reine-de-Course Cri de Coeur via a branch that once belonged to Greentree.  However, in recent years, it appears to have gone down hill and is clearly a Golden Eagle cull.

Negatives:  Raise A Native double counts for unsoundness.  Sire is a “numbers” rather than quality horse and this guy is, after all, a maiden.

*La Troienne influence:  Four lines via Big Event x2/Big Hurry/Businesslike.

 

PINPOINT (KY)            $ 113,291                  Best Beyer 87 (Sir Barton Stakes)

Peaks and Valleys—Identify, by Halo

DP 9-3-13-1-0=26                DI 2.47            CD 0.77         Experimental: None    

Positives:  Tail-female to Reine-de-Course Princess Palatine (same family as Ferdinand, Elusive Quality [sire of Smarty Jones], etc).  Inbred to Almahmoud via Natalma/Cosmah. 

Negatives:  Has not run in, let alone won, a graded race.

*La Troienne influence:  Just one line via Biemelch in sire’s pedigree.

 

REVERBERATE (KY)                      $ 113,935        Best Beyer 106 (Peter Pan Stakes)

Thunder Gulch—Peggibonsi, by Proud Truth

DP 6-0-9-4-1=20                  DI 1.11            CD 0.30         Experimental: None

Positives:  Sire a classic winner (Derby and Belmont) and the sire of a classic winner (Point Given – Preakness and Belmont).  Tail-female to Reine-de-Course family of Lindos Ojos.  Has a great deal of stamina in his pedigree, but might do better on grass.

Negatives:  Couldn’t get close to Oratory in the Peter Pan, couldn’t beat Noble Causeway, who has disappointed. 

*La Troienne influence:  One line via Bimelech in dam’s contribution.

 

 SOUTHERN AFRICA (KY)                  $400,070      Best Beyer 96 (Lone Star Derby)

Cape Town—Al Fahda (GB), by Be My Chief

DP 7-4-9-2-0=22      DP 2.38          CD 0.73         Experimental:  118#

Positives:  Tail-female to Reine-de-Course Chelandry.  Sire has Seattle Slew/Mr. Prospector cross.  Absolutely loaded with Pretty Polly lines.

Negatives:  Everything about his tail-female line says “grass”.  Has not beat good enough dirt horses to know if he really likes the surface.  Seeking the Gold always an unsound factor.

*La Troienne influence:  Baby League x3/Businesslike/Big Event for a total of five crosses.

 

WATCHMON (KY)         $ 47,560      Best Beyer 83 (Allowance)

Maria’s Mon—Watchfull, by Notebook

DP 2-2-7-0-1=12      DI 1.67            CD 0.33         Experimental: None

Positives:  What grass blood he has might help hold him together.

Negatives:  THREE Raise a Native lines!  Tail-female line shows sex bias in favor of good females.

*La Troienne influence:  Three lines via Businesslike x2/Bimelech.
