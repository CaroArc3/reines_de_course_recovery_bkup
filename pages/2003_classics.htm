REINES-DE-COURSE ACE

FIRST FOUR CLASSICS OF SEASON

Reines-de-Course descendents accounted for all four of the first classics in England and the U. S. the weekend of May 3-4.

Kentucky Derby winner Funny Cide and runnerup Empire Maker are both descendents of Reine-de-Course Teresina via her granddaughter Bravura.

Kentucky Oaks winner Bird Town traces to Reine-de-Course Padua via Reine-de-Course Blind Date. Second place finisher Santa Catarina is a descendent of Reine-de-Course Lady Be Good and third place finisher Yell is a descendent of all-powerful Reine *La Troienne.

Two Thousand Guineas winner Refuse To Bend is a descendent of Reine-de-Course Court Dress, while One Thousand Guineas winner Russian Rhythm hails from the impeccable clan of Reine-de-Course Pretty Polly.
