Congratulations To Dimitrova on her victory in the  G1Flower Bowl Invitational at Belmont Park on September 27, 2003

$9.40        $5.70        $2.40

From PEDLINES #89, August 2003

"Our favorite inbred of the month is a grass horse, which should come as no surprise to Pedlines readers, since grass horses are clearly our great love.  It should also come as no surprise that she is inbred to Aloe, our favorite female line.

"Dimitrova is by Swain out of the Caerleon mare The Caretaker.  She hails from the Sweet Marjoram family via Knight's Gal/Saracen Miss.  And one of the things that caught our eye in her pedigree is that The Caretaker's pedigree contains the same Round Table/Saracen Flirt cross that gave us champion Flirting Around, sire of Wolf Power.

"The American Oaks winner is linebred to Aloe via a 7 x 6 x 5 cross of half siblings Above Board/Hypericum/Knight's Daughter, or Feola/Aloe x3.  She also carries a double of *La Troienne via Belle of Troy/Big Hurry at 6 x 7 and a double of Rose Red back to her full sister Sweet Lavender.  It's a gorgeous pedigree.

"Nobody asked us, but lacking any Flirting Around blood, we'd probably do something daring with her first mating, like sending her to Florida for a date with Way West, which would bring in two more Aloe lines, one of which doubles and balances Round Table.  It also gives the resulting foal a 4 x 5 cross of three-quarter sisters Matriarch and Leallah.

"We hope this filly sticks around (or comes back) for the Breeders' Cup Turf Distaff.  Our $2 will be on her for sure."

 

Congratulations To Lunar Sovereign on his victory in the G1Man o' War Stakes at Belmont Park on September 6, 2003

$22.00        $12.80        $5.50

From PEDLINES #89, August 2003:

"Our other horse to watch is not so gloriously bred, but we like him almost as much.  This is the four-year-old Cobra King colt Lunar Sovereign, most recently third in the G1 United Nations behind our old friend Balto Star and The Tin Man.

"Lunar Sovereign traces back to the mare Mexican Tea (1921 by Tea Caddy), ancestress of such horses as Dauphin Fabuleux, Apollo (1988) and Hollywood Derby winner Tumble Wind.  This fellow comes to us via the Kentucky Oaks winner Flying Lill (his fifth dam), whose family has had its share of dry spells but which came to life again with Faultless Tudor (his second dam) who won a whole handful of minor stakes and $208,089.

"Faultless Tudor produced group/graded stakes placed Flawless Image (by The Minstrel) as well as January Moon, the winning dam of Lunar Sovereign.  To date, he has placed in stakes in England, the UAE and now, of course, the U.S.

"His is a most arresting pedigree:  Monarchy and her full brother Round Table appear 4 x 3 and there is a unique double of St. James Palace winner Admiral's Walk (Hyperion--Tabaris by Roi Herode).

"The bottom half of Lunar Sovereign's pedigree has two lovely Maggie B. B. lines via Audience (who is doubled to her) and Mattie T.  The Audience appears via Faultless, the Mattie T. via Lovely Night.

"On the other hand, Cobra King, sire of Lunar Sovereign, also has his own unique double.  His third dam, Sybil's Stand, is inbred to Pretty Polly via Molly Desmond and Polly Flinders (1918).

"Thus each parent has his own inbreeding pattern to a major mare and then the pair combined cross full siblings Round Table and Monarchy.  It's got our attention, and while it may take some convincing to consider this fellow a stallion prospect (there's not much in that area in his lineage), he certainly has racehorse written all over him.  And we're willing to bet that with a bit of luck this fellow will pop up one day and win one of those races he's been placing in.  We'll have our $2 ready."
