Dedicated To The Improvement of the Thoroughbred Bloodline

The articles in this section contain not only analysis of Thoroughbred bloodlines, but commentary as well as criticism.  More importantly they also offer suggestions of both things to avoid in breeding as well as matches that are designed to improve the breed.

Because of this we hope the articles herein will be carefully reviewed and considered by anyone contemplating a Thoroughbred mating.

Your own success and the future of the Thoroughbred is both the goal and the ultimate reward.
