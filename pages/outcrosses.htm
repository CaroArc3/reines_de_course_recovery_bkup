They're (Mostly) Different

We do not claim that our diversity database is complete or that we have every single good horse in the world in here, but we do feel we've made a good start.

If you think you have a horse that is worthy of inclusion (and please, no unraced 20-year-olds standing in Kansas, thank you) we'll certainly consider adding them to the list.  What we are trying for here is a group of horses who are standing somewhere that they might actually have a chance.

This beginning is entirely subjective, but it is a start.  We started with American horses which are not tail-male to either Raise a Native or Northern Dancer and which have little, if any, of that blood.
