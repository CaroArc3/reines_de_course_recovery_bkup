BELLAMY ROAD (FL)     $ 570,000     Best 2005 Beyer 120

Concerto—Hurry Home Hillary, by Deputed Testamony

DP 5-9-8-4-0=26    DI 2.25    CD 0.58   Experimental: 110#

Positives: According to Andy Beyer the 120 Beyer earned in the Wood Memorial (G1) was the best in a Derby prep since the numbers were created in 1991; better than the winning figure in any Triple Crown race during the same period, and better than any horse of any age has earned in the United States this year.

Negatives: Only two starts prior to the Derby.

*La Troienne influence: Businesslike 8D
