About This Site

Ellen Parker, who Leon Rasmussen once dubbed as “Round Table’s Boswell”, has been a pedigree analyst and turf writer for most of her adult life.  She created the Reine-de-Course (“Queens of the Turf) to recognize the influence of female families when no such recognition existed at the time.

Ron Parker has been writing about Thoroughbreds–both seriously and humorously– in various publications for half a century.

Both are long-time members of the National Turf Writers and Broadcasters organization.

Reines – Alphabetical

We are in the process of adding Ellen’s articles naming Reines that originally appeared in Pedlines.
Where the name of a Reine is underlined, simply click on it and the article will appear.
Many Reine names will go to the article in which they were named.  For an extreme example, when *La Troienne was named so were 21 of her descendents.  Thus, clicking on, say, Allemande will take you to the original *La Troienne article.
Recognize that these are the original articles published over the course of a number of years so references to a Thoroughbred who was living at the time may no longer be valid.  But the family histories these articles provide are unmatched anywhere on the web.
