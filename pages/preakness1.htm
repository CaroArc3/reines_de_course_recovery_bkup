 TOP SEVEN (GRADED EARNINGS)

GIACOMO (KY)     $ 1,823.900     Best 2005 Beyer 100 (Kentucky Derby G1)

Holy Bull—Set Them Free, by Stop The Music

DP 5-5-6-0-0=16     DI 4.33     CD 0.94     Experimental: 122#

Positives: Wonderful wake-up call for the breed – he is sans Northern Dancer and Raise A Native.  A real treat for fans of his marvelous sire.

Negatives: Needs to go on proving himself for awhile. Tail-female line quite grassy, might move up on that surface.

*La Troienne influence: Bimelech 6S; Big Event 6S

 

AFLEET ALEX (FL)     $ 1,445,000     Best 2005 Beyer 108 (Arkansas Derby G2)

Northern Afleet—Maggy Hawk, by Hawkster

DP 5-0-9-0-0=14     DI 2.11     CD 0.71     Experimental: 124#

Positives: Dam lacks Raise A Native and Northern Dancer.  Ran his usual consistent race in the Derby.

Negatives: Pedigree full of grass influences – Nureyev, Tentam, Silver Hawk, Hawaii – seems better suited to this surface.

*La Troienne influence: Belle of Troy 7S

 

WILKO (KY)     $ 1,004,515     Best 2005 Beyer 97 (Santa Anita Derby G1)

Awesome Again—Native Roots, by Indian Ridge

DP 4-8-4-0-0=16 DI 7.00 CD 1.00     Experimental: 126# (co-highweight)

Positives: Has shown toughness of bottom line to date.  Ran decently, but hardly brilliantly, in the Derby.

Negatives: Sire not particularly sound. Better suited to grass?

*La Troienne influence: Bimelech 6S

 

HIGH FLY (KY)     $ 796,500     Best 2005 Beyer 102 (Florida Derby G1)

Atticus—Verbasle, by Slewpy

DP 6-4-20-0-0=30     DI 2.00     CD 0.53     Experimental: None

Positives: Slewpy looks to be a good broodmare sire, one of his daughters, Helice, already produced Prix de l’Arc de Triomphe winner Helissio.

Negatives: Sire exiled to California.  Ran inexplicably bad in the Derby.

*La Troienne influence: Bimelech 7D; Baby League 8Dx8D

 

 CLOSING ARGUMENT (FL)            $ 165,000        Best 2005 Beyer 99 (Kentucky Derby G1)

Successful Appeal—Mrs. Greeley, by Mr. Greeley

DP 5-1-4-0-0=10     DI 4.00     CD 1.10        Experimental: 110#

Positives:  Four lines of Reine-de-Course Black Ray (Éclair x2/Jacopo/Sans Lumiere).  Overcame sprinting blood to employ this great linebreeding in the Derby and almost get the job done.  

Negatives:   Lots of sprint blood from Valid Appeal and Groovy, inbreeding to Mr. Prospector plus Reviewer not very sound.

*La Troienne influence:   Two lines via Bimelech and Buinesslike plus a cross of *La Troienne’s three-quarter sister Adargatis via Cornish Prince.

 

HIGH LIMIT (KY)     $ 510,000     Best 2005 Beyer 105 (Louisiana Derby G2)

Maria’s Mon—Known Romance, by Known Fact

DP 3-3-8-0-0=14     DI 2.50     CD 0.64     Experimental: None

Positives: Dam inbred to three-quarter siblings Known Fact and Tentam. Sire has already gotten a Derby winner in Monarchos.

Negatives: Tail-female line could be more solid, though does contain Tim Tam’s old rival Lincoln Road, from the 1958 Derby.  Ran more to the bottom line than the inbreeding in the Derby – a real disappointment at Churchill Downs.

*La Troienne influence: Businesslike 6S; Bimelech 6S

 

SUN KING (KY)     $ 402,500     Best 2005 Beyer 104 (Gulfstream Park allowance race)

Charismatic—Clever But Costly, by Clever Trick

DP 5-3-5-1-0=14     DI 3.00     CD 0.86     Experimental: 123#

Positives: Sire line has strong classic history.

Negatives: Dam cannot claim the same class as the sire, tail-female line a little shaky.  Very disappointing in both the Bluegrass and Derby.

*La Troienne influence: Busanda 6S

 

NEXT FOUR (EARNINGS MINUS RESTRICTED STAKES)

GREELEY’S GALAXY (KY)     $ 358,480     Best 2005 Beyer 106 (Illinois Derby G2)

Mr. Greeley—Ascot Starre, by Ascot Knight

DP 8-4-8-0-0=20     DI 4.00     CD 1.00 Experimental: None

Note: Must pay late nomination fee to enter Kentucky Derby; if 20 other nominations enter he will be excluded (graded earnings do not count for a supplementary entry, so less than 20 must enter for him to be included)

Positives: Nothing to write home about.  Hardly impressed in the Derby.

Negatives: Unsound influences of Mr. Prospector, Reviewer, Danzig and Deputy Minister do not give one hope he’ll be around long. Tail-female line a bit shabby in recent years.

*La Troienne influence: Bimelech 7D; Big Event 7D; Baby League 8D

 

SCRAPPY T (KY)     $ 279,120     Best 2005 Beyer 102 (Withers Stakes G3)

Fit to Fight—Perpetual Light, by Sunny’s Halo

DP 9-5-6-0-0=20     DI 5.67     CD 1.15 Experimental: None

Positives:  Sire is underrated, won Handicap Triple Crown and gets tough, sound runners who last.  Absolutely loaded with Man o’ War blood.  Inbred to the last of the sounder speed blood available via Roman and Bold Ruler.

Negatives:  Has yet to prove himself able in G1 company.  Tail-female line could be more impressive.

*La Troienne influence:  Businesslike/Bimelech via dam only.

 

NOBLE CAUSEWAY (KY)     $ 245,780     Best 2005 Beyer 100 (Florida Derby G1)

Giant’s Causeway—Mimi’s Golden Girl, by Seeking the Gold

DP 6-2-11-0-1=20    DI 2.08    CD 0.60     Experimental: None

Positives: Sire "one of the ones", and this is only his first crop. Inbred to half brothers Chieftain and Tom Rolfe. Solid but not brilliant female line.  Must improve on terrible run at Churchill Downs.

Negatives: Pedigree has grassy look with Storm Bird/Rahy/Roberto/Tom Rolfe and Round Table in the mix, might be better suited to that surface long-term.

*La Troienne influence: Belle of Troy 8S; Businesslike 6Dx7D; Baby League 8D

 

GOING WILD (KY)            $ 229,670        Best 2005 Beyer 100 (Sham Stakes)

Golden Missile—Pola, by Strawberrt Road (AUS)

DP 5-1-9-1-0=16     DI 1.91    CD 0.62        Experimental: None

Positives:  Sire is inbred to Reine-de-Course Imperatrice via Somethingroyal x2/Speedwell.  Sire is inbred to three-quarter sisters Flower Bowl and Your Hostess, both Reines-de-Course.  Is tail-female to *Boudoir II, a Reine-de-Course; third dam is the second dam of Derby and Preakness winner Real Quiet, fourth dam Gay Hostess is the dam of Derby and Preakness winner Majestic Prince.

Negatives:  Recent form, may be better suited to grass, double of *Forli a question for soundness.   Beat 29 lengths in Kentucky.

*La Troienne influence:  x4, all via sire (Baby League x2/Businesslike/Bimelech)

 

FINAL THREE (TOTAL EARNINGS)

GALLOPING GROCER (NY)     $ 222,108     Best 2005 Beyer 93 (Whirlaway Stakes)

A. P Jet—Little Evie, by Northrop

DP 10-8-12-0-0=30     DI 4.00    CD 0.93     Experimental: 119#

Positives:  Brilliant beginning at two.  Tailed off but if he runs to back class, could most definitely be a factor.  Has some nice Tartan blood via Fappiano/In Reality and is sex-balance inbred to Intentionally.  Sire a three-quarter brother to Gone West.

Negatives:  Tail-female line has been known to be a bit fragile.

*La Troienne influence:  Just one line, a cross of Bimelech via Fappiano.

 

MALIBU MOONSHINE (FL)     $ 190,280     Best 2005 Beyer 85 (Private Terms Stakes)

Malibu Moon—Time to Coast, by Coastal

DP 9-2-7-0-0=18     DI 4.14     CD 1.11 Experimental: None

Positives:  By sire of last year’s two-year-old champion, Declan’s Moon.  Three lines of Frizette via Myrtlewood x2/Princess Palatine.  Sire x3 to *Cinq A Sept.

Negatives:  Is stepping way up in class.

*La Troienne influence:  x4 via Baby League x2/Businesslike x2.

 

HAL’S IMAGE (FL)     $ 126,820     Best 2005 Beyer 85 (Aqueduct allowance race)

Halo’s Image—Mia’s Hope, by Rexson’s Hope

DP 9-5-6-0-0=20     DI 5.67     CD 1.15     Experimental: 104#

Positives:  Three-quarter brother to multiple G1 winner Hal’s Hope.  Inbred to three-quarter siblings Halo and Royal Match.  Three crosses of Lavendula (*Perfume II/*Source Sucree/*Ambiorix) also in six-cross. 

Negatives:  Tail-female line could be stronger and to date, has not won or placed in any graded stakes.

*La Troienne influence:  None.
