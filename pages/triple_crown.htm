*La Troienne Influence In Derby Winners

---Ron Parker

One of the most overlooked aspects of pedigrees in classic winners is the enduring presence of super mare *La Troienne (*Teddy-Helene de Trois [FR], by Helicon [GB], 1926) whose influence is still strongly felt in the most modern of pedigrees.

Below are tables for each of the last 17 Kentucky Derbies indicating her influence (if any) in the winners pedigree.  The *La Troienne Source column specifies which of her nine fillies and/or two colts each horse carries, while the S or D column reflects whether this influence comes from the Sire or Dam side of the horse's pedigree.

In an era when pedigrees are glossed over by simple statistics such as easily obtainable sire data, how many winners the dam had, what a horse cost, or the limitations of Dosage numbers, it behooves anyone who truly wants to analyze pedigrees to dig deep into the family to understand and appreciate the historical matings that filter down into any given thoroughbred.
